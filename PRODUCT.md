# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is everyday dog and cat owners in Israel considering a genetic test for their pet, often for the first time. They need to understand what the test can reveal, why the provider is trustworthy, which kit fits their animal, and what happens after ordering.

## Product Purpose

Orivet Israel helps pet owners access at-home DNA testing for dogs and cats. The landing page should build confidence and generate qualified inquiries, while also making kit selection and direct ordering clear.

## Positioning

Orivet Israel connects international genetic laboratory testing with local veterinary expertise, translating genetic results into information pet owners can understand and discuss with their veterinarian.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare tests for dogs and cats, order a kit, collect a non-invasive cheek sample at home, mail it to the Orivet laboratory, and receive results by email and in their online account. Dog test results are stated to arrive within 6–8 weeks after the sample reaches the laboratory.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hebrew, right-to-left static web landing page deployed through Dokploy.
- Preserve the supplied test names, package contents, prices, contact details, and medical disclaimers.
- The three promoted packages are mixed-dog breed identification, mixed-dog breed identification plus health panel, and a breed-specific cat DNA test.
- Cat testing must not claim breed identification or LifePlan.
- Genetic testing provides information about genetic risks and tendencies; it is not a diagnosis and does not replace veterinary consultation.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Orivet Israel name and the established orange and purple brand association. The user supplied binding Hebrew copy and asked that it be used rather than replaced with newly invented copy. The experience should represent both dogs and cats, including young and adult animals, and balance emotional warmth with medical professionalism.

## Product Principles

1. Answer four questions clearly: what the test provides, why it is trustworthy, which package fits, and what happens after ordering.
2. Earn trust through specific, understandable evidence rather than technological spectacle.
3. Make dog and cat paths distinct and factually consistent.
4. Keep veterinary limitations visible without letting disclaimers overwhelm the main decision path.
5. Guide uncertain owners toward a qualified conversation before purchase.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear Hebrew RTL reading order, legible mobile typography, comfortable touch targets, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, meaningful image alternatives, and strong text contrast.
